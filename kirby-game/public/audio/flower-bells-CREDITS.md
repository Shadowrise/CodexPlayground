# Musical flower bells

`flower-bells.wav` contains seven glockenspiel notes (C5, D5, E5, F5, G5,
A5, B5), each 2.05 seconds, packed into one mono 32 kHz / 16-bit WAV.

Source: Versilian Community Sample Library, Versilian Studios LLC.
https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/tree/master/Idiophones/Struck%20Idiophones/Glockenspiel

Original recordings: `glock_soft_C5_02.wav`, `glock_soft_G5_01.wav`,
`glock_soft_C6_01.wav`. Intermediate notes use the nearest recording with
a small pitch shift. Edited for consistent level, short leading silence,
and gentle attack/release. No original background music is included.
