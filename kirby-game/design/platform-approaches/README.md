# Platform and ground approaches

The station foundation core is recessed behind the side and front stone cladding. Previously both side planes were at x=±12, causing depth fighting; the visible platform outline and walking heights are unchanged. A regression test reproduces the duplicate ray intersection before the fix and checks all six facing stones on each side after it.

Removed the 16 decorative pavers in front of the oak ladder and the 22 wooden approach boards and their pegs at the mill. Existing ground paths and the doorway steps remain.

Validation: 16 depot/treehouse/oak/watermill tests, production build, live-game checks on both platform sides and both cleared approaches, no browser errors. Screenshots are from the game, with its normal materials and lighting. No additional geometry, materials, draw passes or frame updates were introduced.
