Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: Beautiful concept illustration for a new separate Meta Knight boss arena in an existing cheerful Kirby 3D game.
Primary request: A large, perfectly circular battle arena floating in open outer space, entered through a magical portal. Match the existing Topotushka arena's generous scale: approximately 72 metres of clear playable diameter and 92 metres overall. The arena is the main subject and must be fully visible.
Scene/backdrop: Deep indigo outer space, rich softly swirling violet and blue nebulae, countless delicately luminous stars, one distant majestic ringed planet and a small moon. Magical, inviting, adventurous cosmic atmosphere.
Arena design: One substantial circular floating disc of midnight-blue polished stone and metal, elegantly bevelled silver outer rim, subtle gold and lavender accents, luminous cyan and violet concentric inlays. Broad open, flat, continuous unobstructed fighting floor with a refined astronomical compass/star pattern and an understated silver Meta Knight mask-and-wings emblem inlaid flush into its centre. Ornamentation feels deliberately designed and restrained. Eight low silver and amethyst perimeter markers and a few glowing crystals integrated into the rim. Beautiful layered underside with violet glowing seams, visibly suspended above the stars. No tall walls obstructing the view.
Portal: A clearly readable upright circular silver magical portal arch with purple-cyan swirling energy stands on a small attached landing at the far perimeter, connected directly to the arena floor by a short broad threshold. Through its centre, a small glimpse of the sunny green meadow from which the player arrives. The portal does not block combat or interrupt the circular fighting floor.
Characters for scale: Tiny pink Kirby near the foreground interior faces tiny Meta Knight farther across the circle, both standing on the floor, with plenty of space between them. They are supporting scale references, each around one-fortieth of the arena diameter, not oversized hero portraits. Meta Knight has a round deep indigo body, silver mask with narrow yellow eyes, violet boots and batlike violet wings, and a golden sword in his right hand.
Style/medium: Exceptionally polished charming high-end stylized 3D game environment concept, softly rounded geometry, detailed clean materials, painterly celestial background, crisp readable architecture, convincing depth and ambient occlusion, graceful luminous highlights with controlled bloom.
Composition/framing: One wide landscape image, elevated three-quarter view looking down enough to show the complete round fighting surface and curved underside. Entire circular platform fits comfortably within the frame with space all around, portal and cosmic background visible. Strong balanced composition, floor brighter and more readable than space, elaborate outer edges but quiet centre.
Lighting: Gentle cool nebula fill, silver rim lighting, beautiful soft purple and cyan glow with small warm gold accents; luminous but no overexposure.
Constraints: An arena concept illustration, no UI, no HUD, no health bars, no typography, no watermark, no labels, no diagram or collage, no inset panels, no buildings or terrain around the platform, no clutter or obstacles in the battle area.
