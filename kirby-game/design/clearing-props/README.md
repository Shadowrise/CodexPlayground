The cottage, maze entrance, four meadow arches and four picnics now use crafted static models. Screenshots in this directory show the actual game with its baked environment, sunlight and shadows.

The cottage has timber panelling, bed joinery, linen trim, flowers, a writing desk, books, a ceramic lamp and a woven chest. Its mattress and pillow retain the sleeping animation's contact points. Picnic blankets have shallow folds and fringes; baskets have woven ribs and an open interior, with porcelain, cutlery, bread, fruit and a flask. Arches use radial stone blocks, stepped bases, fluted columns and folded ivy leaves. The later treehouse follow-up removed the maze's flanking boulders, including their trail reservations; the gateway and paving remain. The original stones screenshot is an earlier review snapshot.

CraftedProps merges static pieces before assigning shared 256px surface maps. Ruin and picnic templates share buffers across every site and feed the existing scenery instancing and culling system. Their colour passes use two and seven meshes respectively. Small petals use cheaper geometry; only useful structural surfaces cast new scenery shadows. No lights, sprites, timers or per-frame decoration updates were added.

Validation: 43 relevant tests passed (sleeping, native character fit, benches, maze, scenery batching, LOD, prepared environment and model budgets), plus the production build. The browser checked every ruin and picnic site, sleeping and waking, a narrow viewport and night lighting; no WebGL or page errors occurred.
